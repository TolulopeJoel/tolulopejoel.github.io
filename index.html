ApproveHR · fairness check

Your manager rated you a 2. Your work says 4. Now someone notices.

This is my favourite thing on ApproveHR, and it's so beautiful. It looks at what people actually did, not who their manager likes, and flags ratings that don't match. No favouritism, whether it's your gender, your age, your department or just your manager.

Look at:

6ways to group people, from gender to department
5people minimum before a group is shown
0names sent to the model

The maths flags it, not the AI. The mismatch comes from evidence the manager doesn't control: measured objectives, peer feedback and past cycles. The AI only writes the note, and every flagged rating needs a decision before it's published.

Kowane · text-to-SQL evals

A query can run, return a number, and still be wrong.

Kowane answers a bank's questions about its own books. If it answers one wrong, confidently, I'm cooked. That's literally the whole product. Most AI apps get to say “AI can make mistakes.” This one doesn't, so I stopped relying on the AI alone and put tests and maths around it.

A case from the suite:

The tempting answer

What passes

34cases through the real agent
14categories, incl. refusal and safety
resultsare compared, not SQL text

And it runs on purpose: repeat runs to catch flaky answers, a baseline that fails the run on any regression, and a workflow to trigger before a prompt change or a model upgrade.
